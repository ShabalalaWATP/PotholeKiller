## Failure recovery

- **API failure:** show the retry message, select **Use a sample instead** and explicitly disclose the predefined output. Never relabel it as live.
- **Map tiles unavailable:** use verified manual coordinates or the saved video. Fictional markers do not verify a real location.
- **Application or venue connection failure:** play the verified 89-second AI-narrated MP4 and identify it as recorded production footage. The 47.68-second WebM is backup only.
- **Clipboard blocked:** open **Read the complete report** and show/select its text.
- **Repeat capture earns 0 XP:** explain the once-only rule. Do not claim another reward was earned.
